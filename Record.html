Different Ways to Make History

Record Categories

Mountaineering records measure many different skills. Each category has its own rules and challenges.

First Ascents

Achievements marking the first documented successful ascent of a mountain or a particular route.

Speed Records

Timed ascents and traverses, where route, starting point, support and timing rules matter.

Without Supplemental Oxygen

High-altitude achievements completed without supplemental oxygen, under the stated record rules.

Winter Ascents

Climbs undertaken during the defined winter season, often involving extreme cold and difficult weather.

Eight-Thousanders

Records involving the world's fourteen mountains whose summits rise above 8,000 metres.

Age and Personal Milestones

Documented youngest, oldest and other personal milestones with clear age and verification criteria.

Base-to-Summit Distinction

Mauna Kea

MEASUREMENT About 10,205 metres from seabed

Mauna Kea in Hawaii is often described as taller when measured from its underwater base to its summit. This is a different measurement from height above sea level.
